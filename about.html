I’m Ivana, or as you might know me online – IVKIPIVKI. I’m a soon-to-be graduate with degree in Graphic Engineering and Design from the Faculty of Technical Sciences in Novi Sad.

My journey in design has been one of passion and exploration, leading me to discover a wide range of creative skills – from web programming and UI/UX design to digital illustration and beyond.

My academic background has helped me build a strong foundation, but it’s my curiosity and love for problem solving that pushes me to continuosly learn and grow.

I find inspiration in understanding the needs of each client and translate those needs into designs that are both functional and visually engaging.
